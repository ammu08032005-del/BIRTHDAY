for my favorite person ♡

To my favorite person,

Sometimes I think about how funny life is. In our first year, I never even noticed you properly. You were just there somewhere around me, and somehow, without either of us realizing it, life kept bringing us closer through friends, random moments and all the little things in between.

The first thing I noticed about you wasn't love. It was your calmness. I am someone who fights over silly things, gets stubborn and gets irritated easily, but you were the first person who never made me feel like I was “too much.”

You never fought back. You were always soft, patient and understanding. And every time I saw you smiling, I used to think, “How can someone be this nice?”

And then came that random Ghibli trend. Who knew one silly edited group photo would change everything for me? That day we started talking properly, and somehow talking to you became my favorite thing.

I felt so comfortable with you so quickly. There wasn't even 1% of a bad feeling in my mind. You just felt safe. Like someone I could tell anything to.

I still remember when you told me you had a crush. My heart was literally like, “whatttt?” Especially because it was my friend.

I listened to you talk about her while quietly hiding my own feelings. I had already started falling for you, but in my mind, you liked someone else, so I thought I would never confess.

But apparently life had other plans for me.

That one conversation where I got jealous and triggered you somehow changed everything. And then you told me you didn't actually like her... you liked me.

I still remember that feeling. I was so shocked and so ridiculously happy at the same time. I kept thinking, “Omg... this calm, sweet boy is going to be my boy.”

And look at us now. ♡

Our first ice cream date still feels so special to me. It was such a simple thing, but I was genuinely so happy.

Then the movie date, where you held my hand for the first time... I honestly cannot explain that feeling properly. It was warm, exciting and comforting — everything at once.

You slowly became part of so many of my dreams without even trying.

The ring I gave you was something I had kept for years without knowing who it belonged to. And then one day, I realized it was meant for you all along.

Spending those six days together during placement training, being in the same group while all our friends were somewhere else, made me feel even closer to you.

And Goa... oh God, Goa will always stay in my heart.

Riding that scooter with you, hugging you tightly while you drove, irritating you by removing my hand sometimes just to hear you complain... those moments genuinely felt unreal.

Like I was inside one of my own dreams.

You know what I love the most? It isn't even the big things. It's the little things.

You staying back in the hostel for one extra day just because my exam was later.

You coming to my home and spending time with me there.

You taking me out after college just so we could spend a little more time together.

You listening to me endlessly... even when I'm doing my usual blah blah blah.

You being patient with me.

You making ordinary days feel special without even trying.

Even the bouquet I made for Valentine's Day wasn't perfect, but it came from my heart, just like every feeling I have for you.

And now when I look at my life, I realize how happy you have made me. You came into my life so quietly, but now you are one of the biggest reasons behind my smile.

Somehow, now I'm at that point where if something good happens, you're one of the first people I want to tell. And when something bad happens, you're still the person I want beside me.

I don't know what I did to deserve someone as kind, caring and genuine as you, but I'm grateful every single day that I met you.

Thank you for being my comfort person, my safe place, my happiest memory, and the calm to my stubbornness.

Thank you for holding my hand. I don't think you realize how much I love that little thing. Maybe because it's such a small thing, but somehow it always makes me feel like everything is okay.

And yes, I will continue irritating you, making fun of you, stealing your attention, and probably trying to bite you because you're too cute. That's just part of the package now. :)

I don't know exactly what the future is going to look like, but I know what I want. I want a happy life with you.

A simple, nice life filled with stupid conversations, random plans, holding hands, annoying each other, laughing at things that aren't even funny, and making many more memories together.

I hope we get to grow together, experience more places, celebrate more birthdays, have more late-night conversations, and look back one day and realize how much we've been through together.

So, Dariuz... happy birthday to the boy who somehow went from “just someone I knew in college” to one of the most important people in my life.

I love you so much. More than these words can ever explain.

— Parvathy ♡
